About AI Market

AI Market was created to make AI-powered content creation simple, affordable and accessible for Indian and global creators.

Our Goal

Our goal is to bring powerful AI tools to creators, agencies, freelancers and small businesses without forcing them to learn complex software. If you can use a browser, you can use AI Market.

From reels and thumbnails to music and templates, AI Market aims to become your one-stop platform for day-to-day content production.

Who Is AI Market For?

  • YouTubers and short video creators
  • Instagram Reels and Shorts creators
  • Freelancers and editors serving local clients
  • Agencies and studios working with high volume content
  • Communities, NGOs and social projects

How We Work

We experiment with AI models, automations and workflows, then wrap them into easy-to-use tools and templates. We actively listen to user feedback and keep iterating our features.

If you have a feature or integration idea, please reach out via the Contact page or WhatsApp.
