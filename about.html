About AI Market

AI Market is an experimental platform to create AI-powered videos, images and music for creators and businesses.

The idea behind AI Market is simple: many people have great ideas, songs and stories, but not everyone has time, hardware or software skills to edit professional content. We use AI + manual editing to bridge that gap.

We focus on:

  • Helping small creators and local businesses with affordable content.
  • Experimenting with new AI workflows and templates.
  • Providing simple WhatsApp-based ordering instead of complex dashboards.

AI Market is still evolving. Features, pricing and tools may change as we learn what works best. Feedback is always welcome.
