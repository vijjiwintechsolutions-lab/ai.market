Refund & Cancellation Policy

Please read this policy carefully before purchasing a plan or service from AI Market.

1. Digital Services

AI Market provides access to digital tools and services. In most cases, access is granted immediately or shortly after payment. Because of this, we generally do not offer refunds once a plan is activated.

2. When Refunds May Be Considered

A refund may be considered in special cases, such as:

  • Duplicate payment for the same plan.
  • Technical issues that completely prevent usage and cannot be resolved.
  • Accidental payment for a wrong plan, if reported immediately.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us via WhatsApp or email with the following details:

  • Full name and contact number
  • Date and time of payment
  • Razorpay payment reference ID
  • Reason for refund request

4. Processing Time

If approved, refunds will be processed through the original payment method within 7–10 working days, depending on bank or gateway.

5. No Guarantee of Refund

Submitting a refund request does not guarantee approval. AI Market reserves the right to decide whether a refund is appropriate based on usage history, time and reason.

6. Plan Changes

Instead of refunds, in some cases we may offer plan extensions, credits or upgrades. Our goal is always to help you get the best value.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact us via the Contact page.
