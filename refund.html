Refund & Cancellation Policy

Please read this policy before placing orders. Digital content is different from physical products.

1. Digital Nature of Service

AI Market delivers customised digital outputs (videos, images, audio). Once work is started and delivered, it cannot be “returned” like a physical item.

2. No Refund After Final Delivery

In general, payments for completed and delivered work are non-refundable. We may provide small edits or corrections if there is any clear issue from our side.

3. Cancellation Before Work Starts

If you made a payment but no work has started yet, you can request cancellation. We will review and may refund fully or partially, depending on payment charges and discussion.

4. Technical or Payment Errors

If money is deducted but payment is not shown as successful, please share the transaction details and we will coordinate with the payment gateway. Final decision may depend on Razorpay or your bank.
