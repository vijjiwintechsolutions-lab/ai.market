Refund Policy

Last updated: 2025

General Policy

AI Market tools and plans are digital services. Once a plan is activated and used, it may not be fully refundable. However, we try to be reasonable in genuine cases.

Payment Failures & Duplicates

If money was debited from your bank or UPI but Razorpay shows payment as failed or pending, please wait for automatic reversal from your bank. In case of duplicate charges, contact us with your Razorpay payment ID and we will verify the status.

Eligibility for Refund

  • Incorrect amount charged due to technical error.
  • Duplicate payment for the same plan or period.
  • Plan not activated after successful payment (and you no longer wish to use it).

Non-Refundable Situations

  • Plan has been used fully or significantly.
  • Misuse of tools, violation of Terms & Conditions or suspicious activity.
  • Change of mind after using the service.

How to Request a Refund

Please contact us via Contact page or WhatsApp with:

  • Your name and contact number
  • Plan name (Basic / Pro / Premium / Donation)
  • Razorpay payment ID and payment date

After reviewing the request, we will inform you about the eligibility and next steps as per this policy.
