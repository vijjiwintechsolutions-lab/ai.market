Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains when refunds may be considered for digital services purchased from AI Market.

1. Digital Services

Our services are primarily digital (AI tools access, generated videos, images and audio). Once a plan is activated or content is delivered, it generally cannot be “returned”.

2. When Refunds May Be Considered

Refunds may be considered only in limited cases, for example:

  • Payment was charged but you did not receive tool access or delivery, and the issue cannot be fixed.
  • We are unable to provide the service due to an internal or technical issue on our side.

3. When Refunds Are Not Available

Refunds are usually not provided in cases such as:

  • Change of mind after purchase or activation.
  • Delays caused by incomplete inputs, slow communication or platform limitations.
  • Personal preference about style when work matches the agreed scope.

4. Revision Instead of Refund

Wherever possible, we will try to correct or improve the delivered content within the revision limits of your plan, instead of issuing a refund.

5. Payment Gateway Charges

Third-party payment gateways like Razorpay may charge transaction fees. In most cases, such fees are non-refundable. Approved refunds may be limited to the net amount received.

6. How to Request a Refund

To discuss a refund, contact us with your payment details, plan name and the issue you faced. We will review the request and respond with available options.
