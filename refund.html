Refund & Cancellation Policy

Please read this policy before placing orders or making donations.

Language & worldwide use: This policy is provided in English and applies to all AI Market users worldwide. If we provide translations, the English version will control in case of conflict.

1. Digital Nature of Services

AI Market delivers customised digital content (such as videos, images and audio). Once work has been created and delivered, it cannot be returned like a physical product.

2. No Refund After Final Delivery

In general, payments for completed and delivered work are non-refundable. We may provide reasonable corrections or small edits where there is a clear issue from our side.

3. Cancellation Before Work Starts

If you have made a payment but we have not yet started work on your order, you may request cancellation. We will review the situation and may provide a full or partial refund, depending on payment gateway charges and any time already spent.

4. Technical or Payment Errors

If money has been deducted from your account but payment status is unclear, please share the transaction details (screenshot, transaction ID, date and time). We will coordinate with the payment gateway or request that you contact your bank if needed.

5. Donations

Donations made via the Donate page are voluntary and are generally non-refundable. If a technical error occurs, we will try to assist, but final decisions may depend on the payment provider and bank policies.
