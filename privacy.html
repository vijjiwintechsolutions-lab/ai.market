Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Market collects, uses and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect basic details such as your email address, name and login information when you create an account or sign in using Google or GitHub. We may also store information about the plans or tools you use.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Communicate updates, support messages or important notices

3. Data Storage & Security

We use cloud services like Firebase and trusted payment gateways to store data in a secure way. However, no system is 100% secure, so we recommend you use a strong password and avoid sharing sensitive data unnecessarily.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as Firebase Authentication, Razorpay, Google Analytics or other tools integrated by Google Tag Manager. These services have their own privacy policies and may collect information such as device type, IP address and usage behavior.

5. Cookies & Tracking

This site may use cookies or similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors use AI Market. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain account and transaction information for as long as reasonably necessary for legal, tax and support reasons. You can request account deletion, but some records may be kept as required by law.

7. Your Choices

You can update your login methods (for example, using Google or GitHub) in their respective account settings. If you wish to delete your AI Market account or have questions about your data, contact us using the details on the Contact page.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of AI Market means you accept the new policy.
