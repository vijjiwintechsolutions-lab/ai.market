Privacy Policy

This policy explains how AI Market handles your information. It may be updated from time to time.

We may receive information when you:

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Communicate with you about orders and support.

3. Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay or similar payment providers. We do not store your full card details on our own servers.

4. Sharing of Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with:

Project files and messages may be kept for a reasonable period for backup and support. You can request deletion of specific files by contacting us, but backups may remain for some time.

6. Cookies & Analytics

We may use cookies and tools like Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Contact
