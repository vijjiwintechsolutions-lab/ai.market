We may collect information when you:

We use collected information to:

3. Payments

Payments are processed by third-party providers such as Razorpay. We do not store your full card details on our servers. We may receive limited information about the transaction status to confirm your order.

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with:

We may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences (such as dark/light mode) and to understand how visitors use the site. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Third-party tools like Google Analytics or Tag Manager may also set cookies.

AI Market can be accessed from many countries. Regardless of where you are located, by using the Service you agree that your information may be processed in India and other locations where we or our providers operate. We will take reasonable steps to protect your information as described in this Policy.

You may choose to limit the personal details you share with us. However, certain information may be needed to provide services (for example contact details to send you the final output).
