Cookies Policy

This page explains how AI Market may use cookies and similar technologies.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help remember certain information about your visit.

2. How We Use Cookies

We may use cookies to:

  • Remember basic preferences (like dark/light mode).
  • Understand how visitors use the site via analytics tools.
  • Help integrate third-party services like Google Tag Manager.

3. Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to control or delete cookies through settings. If you disable cookies entirely, some parts of the site may not function perfectly, but basic pages should still work.

4. Third-Party Cookies

External services (for example Google Analytics, payment gateways) may use their own cookies. We do not control those cookies directly, but they are generally used for analytics and security.
