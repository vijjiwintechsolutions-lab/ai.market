How AI Market uses cookies and similar technologies.

Language & worldwide use: This policy is provided in English and applies to all AI Market users worldwide. If we provide translations, the English version will control in case of conflict.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help remember your preferences and improve your experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

We may use cookies to:

  • Remember basic preferences (such as dark/light mode).
  • Understand how visitors use the site through analytics tools.
  • Support the functioning of third-party integrations like Google Tag Manager.

3. Third-Party Cookies

External services, including analytics and payment providers, may place their own cookies. We do not control these third-party cookies directly but they are generally used for security, analytics and payment processing.

4. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control or delete cookies via settings. If you disable cookies entirely, some features of the site may not function as intended, but basic pages should still be usable.
