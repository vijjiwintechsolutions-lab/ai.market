What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit. They help websites remember your preferences and understand how you use the site.

How AI Market Uses Cookies

  • To remember basic preferences such as theme selection.
  • To collect anonymous analytics data to improve the website.
  • To help secure forms and prevent spam or abuse.

Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services, such as Google Analytics, may set their own cookies for analytics and performance.

Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to manage or block cookies in their settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.
