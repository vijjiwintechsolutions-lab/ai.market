Cookies Policy

This page explains how AI Market uses cookies and similar technologies.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make sites work, remember preferences and provide analytics.

2. How We Use Cookies

AI Market may use cookies to:

  • Remember your theme preference (dark / light).
  • Maintain basic session or authentication state.
  • Measure site traffic and usage patterns.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We may use analytics or tag manager services (like Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager). These services may place their own cookies to track aggregated usage.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you disable cookies, some features of AI Market may not function properly.

5. Updates

This Cookies Policy may be updated from time to time. Please revisit this page regularly to stay informed.
