1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your actions and preferences over a period of time.

We may use cookies to:

Some cookies may come from third-party services like Google Analytics, Firebase or payment gateways. These services have their own privacy and cookies policies.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies using your browser settings. If you disable certain cookies, some features of AI Market may not work correctly (such as staying logged in or saving preferences).
