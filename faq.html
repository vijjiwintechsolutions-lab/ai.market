Frequently Asked Questions

Some quick answers to common questions about AI Market plans, tools and payments. If you still need help, message us via WhatsApp or use the Contact page.

Which plan should I choose – Basic, Pro or Premium?

If you are experimenting or using AI Market casually, Basic is enough. For regular creators, freelancers and editors, Pro is recommended. If you run an agency or need higher limits for clients, choose Premium.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes. You can move from Basic to Pro or Premium later. We will adjust the remaining validity of your old plan on a fair-use basis.

How are payments handled?

All payments shown on this site are handled securely through Razorpay payment links. We do not store your card, UPI or banking details on this website.

Are payments refundable?

Please read our Refund Policy for detailed terms. In general, once a plan is fully used or misused, refunds may not be possible.

Do you support regional languages?

Yes. Many of our tools are optimised for Indian languages, especially for audio, songs and dialogue-based content. We are continuously adding more support.

How can I contact support?

You can use the WhatsApp button on any page, or send a message through the Contact page. Please include your plan type and payment ID (if applicable).

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about AI Market, pricing, tools and usage.

1. What is AI Market?
AI Market is a collection of AI-powered tools for video, audio, images, thumbnails, music and automation. Instead of using many different sites, you can work from a single platform.
2. Do I need to install any software?
No. AI Market works completely online. You only need a browser and internet connection.
3. How does the pricing work?
We provide three main plans – Basic, Pro and Premium. Each plan has different limits and access to tools. You can see the full comparison on the Pricing page.
4. Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Yes. You can move from Basic to Pro or Premium anytime by using the respective unlock page. For downgrades, please contact us via WhatsApp.
5. Do you support Indian languages?
Yes. Many of our voice, video and template tools support English, Telugu, Hindi, Tamil and more. We keep adding new languages based on requests.
6. Can I use AI Market content for YouTube or clients?
In general yes – you can use the generated content for your own channels and projects. However, follow platform rules and avoid using copyrighted inputs (music, movies, etc.) you don’t own.
7. What payment methods are supported?
Payments are processed securely via Razorpay. You can pay using UPI, net banking, cards and supported wallets. We do not store card details on AI Market.
8. Where can I get help if I am stuck?
We provide WhatsApp support for plan selection, tool usage and troubleshooting. Visit the Contact page for details.

Still have questions? Send your doubts on WhatsApp or email from the Contact page, and we will reply as soon as possible.
