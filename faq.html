Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about how AI Market works, payments and delivery.

1. Do I need to create an account?

Right now, no login is required. You can open the website, choose a plan, pay and then send details via WhatsApp.

2. How do I place an order?

Go to the Pricing page, select a plan, pay via Razorpay or click “Order on WhatsApp”. After payment, send your song / script / photos to our WhatsApp number with screenshot.

3. How will I receive the final output?

We normally deliver via WhatsApp or a download link (Google Drive, etc.) depending on file size.

4. How long will it take?

Delivery time depends on queue and complexity. Small reels or thumbnails are usually delivered within a few hours to 1 day. Bigger projects may take longer – we will update you on WhatsApp.

5. Are payments secure?

Yes. Online payments are processed via Razorpay (UPI, Cards, Netbanking, Wallets). We do not store your card details.

6. Can I get a refund?

Please read our Refund Policy. In short, digital work already completed and delivered is usually non-refundable, but we will try to help if there is any genuine issue.

7. How can I contact support?

Visit the Contact page or message us directly on WhatsApp: +91 96761 42165.
