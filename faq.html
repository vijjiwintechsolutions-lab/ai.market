Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do I need to log in to use AI Market?

Right now, you can browse all pages without logging in. For future features like coupons, saved projects and redemptions, login may be required.

2. How does the unlock system work?

Choose a plan on the Pricing page and pay using Razorpay. After successful payment, we’ll send unlock details by WhatsApp or email.

3. Can I get a refund?

Please read our Refund Policy. In general, we don’t refund already used credits, but we’ll try our best if something goes wrong.

4. What languages do you support?

We focus on Indian languages such as Telugu, Tamil and Hindi, along with English.

5. How do I contact support?

Use the WhatsApp button on any page or visit the Contact page.
