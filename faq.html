Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I start using AI Market?

Log in, go to the Pricing page, choose a plan and complete payment. After payment you will be redirected to an unlock page for your tools.

2. Is everything fully automated?

Some tools are automated, others are semi-manual with AI assistance. We may review outputs before final delivery to maintain quality.

3. Do I need a powerful PC?

No. AI Market runs in the cloud. You can create and manage content from mobile or simple laptops.

4. What languages do you support?

We primarily support English, Telugu, Tamil and Hindi content. Other languages can be discussed on request.

5. Can I cancel or get a refund?

Since services are digital, refunds are limited. See the Refund Policy for details.

6. How do I contact support?

Use the options on the Contact page. WhatsApp support is available during normal working hours.
